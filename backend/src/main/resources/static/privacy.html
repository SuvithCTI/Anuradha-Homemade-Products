Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This policy details how we collect and use your details.

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us (e.g. First Name, Last Name, and Email Address) when registering for an account, subscribing to newsletters, or placing organic orders.

2. Information Usage

We utilize your registration details strictly to authenticate logins, verify registration details via confirmation emails, process purchase checkout details, and communicate relevant updates.

3. Data Protection

All sensitive authentication passwords are encrypted using strong BCrypt hashing algorithms before database storage. We implement TLS/SSL encryptions for communications.

4. Cookies & Remember Me

Our website utilizes secure cookies to support login sessions. Selecting "Remember Me" activates secure persistent cookie storage configurations.
