Purity You Can Trust, Quality You Can Taste

At Anuradha Homemade Organics, our journey began with a simple purpose: to feed our children pure, chemical-free food. Traditional health recipes hold incredible nutritive strength that modern commercial processing often strips away.

That is why we prepare all our products using traditional techniques. Our ingredients are locally sourced from organic farmers, hand-selected, sun-dried, sprouted naturally, and roasted lightly in small batches to preserve natural nutrition, freshness, and aroma.

Our Production Philosophy

Every spoonful of our Sprouted Nuts Powder and Sathu Maavu tells a story of patience. Unlike factory production, we do not roast our ingredients at extreme temperatures to speed up the process. Instead, we dry our millets under sun sheets and slow-roast them individually to prevent the destruction of heat-sensitive micro-nutrients.

Our ghee is made using the Vedic Bilona method, which requires churning whole curd rather than milk cream. This guarantees a rich, grainy texture and traditional therapeutic properties that support digestion and joint lubrication.
