Our Traditional Heritage

Scroll down to trace our time-tested organic process

01

Organic Sourcing

We source grains, wild gooseberries, and nuts directly from chemical-free farmers who practice crop rotation and soil revitalization.

02

Natural Sprouting

Sprouting Finger Millets (Ragi) locks in nutrition and increases iron and calcium bioavailability by up to 300% for easier digestion.

03

Hygienic Sun Drying

Grains are spread out under clean UV sheets to dry slowly in normal sunlight, preserving all delicate heat-sensitive enzymes.

04

Traditional Woodfire Roasting

Ingredients are slow-roasted in heavy iron pans. This caramelizes the natural starches and releases a deep, authentic aroma.

05

Vedic Bilona Churning

Our ghee is prepared from whole milk curd, hand-churned in clay pots, and clarified slowly to produce grainy, therapeutic ghee.
